## About Me

I’m a Research Scientist at Meta Superintelligence Labs working on PyTorch.

Prior to Meta, I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA under the supervision of [Prof.Bahram Jalali](https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/bahram-jalali/). My research there centered on physics-informed machine learning and physics-inspired algorithms, including novel neural architectures and real-time computational imaging systems. Prior to UCLA, I got my B.E. in Eletronic Information Engineering from [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/).
